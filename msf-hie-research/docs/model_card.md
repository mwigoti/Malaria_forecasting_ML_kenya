# Model card

## Intended use

This forecasting workflow is intended to support operational health planning and epidemiological monitoring. Outputs may be used as a signal for seasonal planning, hotspot prioritization, and resource allocation discussions.

## Limitations

- Model performance is sensitive to data quality and coverage
- Historical patterns may not generalize to unusual outbreaks or policy shocks
- Spatial and environmental covariates require careful validation

## Monitoring

Track stability of key metrics over time and monitor any drift in input data quality, case definition, and reporting completeness.
