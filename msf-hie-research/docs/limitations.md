# Limitations

This analysis should be interpreted as a decision-support tool rather than a deterministic forecast.

Key caveats include:

- incomplete or delayed reporting can bias incidence estimates
- environmental covariates may be proxies for underlying processes
- spatial models may be affected by differences in administrative boundaries and access to care
- machine learning models can be highly sensitive to train/test splits and feature engineering choices

These limitations should be acknowledged in all operational and research summaries built from this project.
